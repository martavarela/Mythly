Poseidon

Roman form: Neptune

Poseidon was the son of the Titans Kronos and Rhea and the god of the sea, earthquakes, and horses in Greek mythology. He was one of the twelve Olympian gods and was often depicted with a trident, which he used to stir up storms and cause earthquakes. He had a son named Triton, who was an immortal being with two fishtails. Poseidon states that when one becomes too consumed by power, they build their own ruin.
