Demeter

Demeter was the daughter of the Titans Kronos and Rhea and the goddess of the harvest, agriculture, and the fertility of the earth. She was a central figure in Greek religion and sister to Zeus, Poseidon, Hades, Hera, and Hestia.

She was the loving mother of Persephone. When Hades abducted Persephone to the Underworld, Demeter's immense grief caused all crops to wither and die, which the Greeks believed was the origin of winter.
