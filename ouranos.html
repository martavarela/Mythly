Ouranos

Type: Primordial

Roman form: Caelus

Who he is

Ouranos is the primordial god of the sky. Born from Gaia, he became her husband and covered her every night, ruling as the first king of the cosmos.

Family and myths

He fathered the Titans, the Cyclopes and the Hundred-Handers with Gaia. Disgusted by the Hundred-Handers and Cyclopes, he hid them inside Gaia, causing her great pain. Kronos ambushed him with an adamantine sickle and castrated him; from the blood that fell came the Giants, the Furies, and, in some tellings, Aphrodite born from the sea foam. Ouranos then prophesied that Kronos' own children would overthrow him.

Philosophy

Ouranos is the first example in Greek myth of a ruler who tries to stop his children from growing and is destroyed by them. His story is about the cycle of power: those who hold onto it through fear and suppression create the very rebellion they dread.
