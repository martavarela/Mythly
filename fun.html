      Myth Top Trumps

      Play a quick Top Trumps duel against the AI.

      Open in full screen

      How to play

      • Each round, you and the AI each reveal one card.
      • Sometimes the AI chooses the category, and sometimes you choose it.
      • When it is your turn to choose, click the category you want to compare, such as Strength or Wisdom.
      • The winner of the round gets to choose the category for the next round.
      • After a category is chosen, both cards are compared using that stat.
      • All categories are scored out of 10.
      • The higher value wins the round and the winning player takes the cards.
      • If the values are the same, those cards are set aside and the next card from each player is revealed. They use the same category as they did in the tied round. The person who has the higher value that time wins the cards played that round and in the tied round(s).
      • Keep playing until one player has no cards left, and that player wins the game.
